Good evening, and welcome to the city news at 7. Heavy rain lashed Kolkata for the third day in a row on Tuesday, and the weather office has issued an orange alert until Friday. More than 120 millimetres of rain was recorded at Alipore between midnight and noon. Several low-lying areas, including parts of Behala and Salt Lake, were waterlogged, and traffic on the EM Bypass moved at a crawl for nearly two hours.
The Metro Railway said that services on the Blue Line ran normally, but three suburban trains from Sealdah were cancelled. Schools in the district will remain closed tomorrow, the education department announced this afternoon.
In other news, a young software engineer from Durgapur, Priya Sen, has won a national coding competition held in Bengaluru. Twenty-four-year-old Priya beat more than 8,000 participants and received a prize of two lakh rupees. Speaking to reporters, she said she hopes to build free learning apps for students in rural Bengal.
Finally, the ticket counters for the Durga Puja special trains will open on the 15th of October at 8 in the morning. Passengers are requested to book online where possible. That's all for now. Stay safe, and thank you for watching.
