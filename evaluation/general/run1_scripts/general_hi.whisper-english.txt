Namaste friends! Today I am going to tell you the most special recipe of my grandmother. Potato Parathas. This recipe is for 4 people. First of all, boil 4 medium-sized potatoes and let them cool. Then peel the potatoes and mash them well. Add 1 finely chopped onion, 2 green chillies, a little green coriander, 1/2 tsp red chilli powder, 1/2 tsp garam masala and salt to taste.
Knead the dough. In 2 cups of wheat flour, add a little salt and 1 tsp oil and mix the water slowly to make a soft dough. Cover the dough for 15 minutes. After this, take a ball of the dough. Roll it a little. In the meantime, keep 2 tsp of potato masala and close it from all sides. Now roll the paratha lightly. Heat the pan and fry the paratha from both sides until golden brown.
Grease the pan with some ghee. Serve the paratha hot with curd, pickle or green chutney. If you like this recipe, then like the video and don't forget to subscribe to the channel. See you next week with a new recipe. Thank you!
